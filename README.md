# Fast Fingers: Code Sprint Edition
#### Sharpen your coding keystrokes, one word at a time! Current Version: May 2, 2025
#### By **Jeremy Amani** (Sole Contributor)

## Description
**Tired of your typing speed holding back your coding flow?** Fast Fingers: Code Sprint Edition is the exhilarating web-based game designed to transform beginner software engineers from hesitant peckers to lightning-fast typists. Dive into a dynamic word challenge that not only tests your speed but also familiarizes you with common programming keywords and terminology. Track your progress, witness your accuracy soar, and conquer the lag that slows down your development journey. With built-in user accounts to save your history, you can chart your evolution from coding novice to typing pro. Get ready to unleash your inner coding speed demon!

## Setup/Installation Requirements
* Launch your coding journey by opening `index.html` in your preferred web browser.
* Navigate to the high-octane typing arena via the "PLAY GAME" link (`./game.html`).
* Create your coder profile or log back in through the "REGISTER" (`./register.html`) and "Login" (`./login.html`) links on the homepage.
* No complex server setups or dependency wrangling required – just pure browser power and your fingertips!

## Known Bugs
{At this stage, no critical bugs have been identified that would significantly impede your training. However, keep an eye out for potential minor visual glitches or unexpected behavior across different browser versions. Your feedback is invaluable in squashing any lingering typos! }

## Technologies Used
* **HTML:** The structural backbone of your typing practice environment.
* **CSS:** Styling the interface to be both engaging and easy on the eyes (`./Styles/index.css`). Featuring the retro charm of the "Press Start 2P" font.
* **JavaScript:** The engine powering the real-time gameplay, user interaction, dynamic feedback, local data storage (`./src/Script.js`), and even a bit of homepage flair.
